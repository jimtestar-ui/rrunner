# Traffic Quips

Short labels for the destination cards. Keep these fast to read because the driver is using the app before accepting a ride, delivery, or order.

## Green

- Smooth Sailing
- Good to Go
- Clear Run
- Open Road
- Easy Move
- Green Light
- All Clear
- Fast Lane
- No Delay
- Ride Ready

## Yellow

- Worth a Look
- Detour Smart
- Route Around
- Side Street Save
- Dodge the Jam
- Plan B Pays
- Workaround Win
- Better Path
- Reroute Ready
- Sneak Through

## Red

- No Go Zone
- Money Trap
- Hard Pass
- Stay Away
- Dead Stop
- Time Sink
- Profit Killer
- Total Jam
- Not Worth It
- Road Blocked

## Stale Or Unknown

- Check Needed
- Old Intel
- Refresh First
- Fresh Check
- Needs Update
